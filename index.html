What this is

We went through Stompers the way a new Stomper does, then rebuilt the screen where Super is decided.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion across the market, not inside one app. We went through your iOS onboarding, from “Howdy! What’s your name?” and “Who do you want to use Stompers with?” to “Choose your Stomper” and “Choose your top”, on to the Super Stompers paywall, and checked every price against what the paywall and your App Store listing show.

It is a fun, honest paywall in your own world: the desert, the outlined type, seven perks with their own art and a real review. What it does not do is use what Stompers just learned. Mary picked a pink Stomper in a black sweater and said she wants to walk with all her friends. The paywall shows a yellow stranger, lists the perks in the same order for everyone, keeps your rating three scrolls down, and prices two plans without saying per what.

We did not redesign your paywall

The desert hero, the outlined headline and your 2.5x line, the white plan card with the orange tick and the yellow “POPULAR” badge, the perk cards, the review, and the orange button with the black outline all stay. Only the Stomper, the words, the order and one small sheet change.

Paywall rebuild · October 2026

The Super Stompers paywall, finishing what the onboarding started

Same screen, same art direction, same prices. Five changes, each fed by something Stompers already asks, already sells or already publishes. Flip Today above the phone to see the screen as it ships.

1

Their own Stomper on the paywall

Today
A yellow walker and People who have Super Stompers walk 2.5x more, the same for everyone.
Now
Uses: the name from “Howdy! What’s your name?”, the colour from “Choose your Stomper” and the top from “Choose your top”, walking across your own desert with your rock and bush. Your 2.5x line stays word for word.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +15–20%to trial
2

The perk that fits who they walk with

Today
Seven perks in one fixed order, starting with 24 hr mode, whatever they answered.
Now
Uses: the answer to “Who do you want to use Stompers with?” and your seven perk cards, art and words unchanged; only the order and one line under the headline change.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +10–15%to trial
3

Your rating, under the button and up to date

Today
Loved by thousands of Stompers like you, 4.7 from 1.7k reviews, three scrolls below the button.
Now
★ 4.8 · 13K ratings · 1,000,000+ Stompers right under the button and the price line; the block at the bottom shows the same, current numbers.
Uses: 4.8 from 13K ratings on your US listing (Spanish screen: 4.7 from your Spanish listing’s ratings) and “Joining our 1,000,000+ member community” from your own onboarding.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +10–15%to trial
4

Every price says per what

Today
Annual 44,99 € with 3,74 €/mo; Monthly 9,99 €; Weekly 5,99 €. The button says Try 7 days for free over then 44,99 €/year.
Now
Uses: your three prices and your POPULAR · SAVE €74.89 badge. 44,99 € over 52 weeks is 0,87 € a week; 9,99 € a month is 2,31 € a week.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataARPU +8–15%more people on Annual
5

A month of Super when they close

Today
The X closes the paywall. No second offer.
Now
Uses: your own Monthly plan at 9,99 €, no trial, and five of your perks for the comparison. A different plan, not a discount on Annual; no countdown.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +3–6%from people who would have closed
